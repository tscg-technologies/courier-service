Category A

Same-Day Courier

Conceptual rapid-response courier service prioritizing immediate point-to-point transport for time-critical packages within designated metropolitan municipal boundaries.

Suitable Requirement: Urgent tenders, replacement parts, emergency medical diagnostics, passports, or critical business correspondence requiring transit within hours.
Operational Process: Sender initiates request prior to daily operational cut-off times. A direct field courier is dispatched for single-leg pickup and direct recipient handover.
* Illustrative model. Acceptance depends on booking timestamp, traffic conditions, package dimensions, and local zone limits. No guaranteed arrival time is claimed.

Category B

Express Courier

High-priority parcel transit leveraging overnight air linehaul flights and expedited surface express lanes for time-sensitive consignments.

Suitable Requirement: Priority commercial merchandise, important e-commerce shipments, and time-critical inter-city packages requiring next available transit slots.
Operational Process: Parcels are flagged with priority barcodes, processed through fast-track sortation conveyors, and loaded onto the earliest outgoing linehaul connection.
* Priority handling is subject to airport cargo cut-offs, flight schedules, and weather conditions.

Standard Courier Delivery

Cost-effective, reliable scheduled delivery balancing budget efficiency with dependable tracking and careful package stewardship.

Suitable Requirement: Non-urgent personal gifts, seasonal consignments, routine office replenishment, and household parcel dispatches.
Operational Process: Consignments move through standard regional consolidation hubs and ground logistics networks with predictable transit cadence.
Book Standard Delivery
Category D

Document Courier

Specialized protocol for confidential paper records, legal briefs, tax filings, real estate titles, and corporate contracts.

Suitable Requirement: Attorneys, accountants, corporate secretarial offices, universities, and administrative bodies.
Operational Process: Envelopes are sealed in serial-numbered tamper-evident pouches, scanned at custody handoffs, and delivered against mandatory digital receiver signature.
* Document acceptance depends on compliance with statutory privacy laws and standard carrier terms. Identity proof may be requested upon handover.

Domestic Parcel Delivery

Comprehensive inter-state and intra-state parcel logistics linking commercial districts, suburban clusters, and regional transit hubs.

Suitable Requirement: Medium-to-large consumer packages, retail replenishment stock, and inter-city personal dispatches.
Operational Process: Packages are routed through multi-modal trunk linehaul transit with systematic hub arrival, staging, and final-mile branch handoff.

Business Courier Services

Structured corporate courier relationships offering scheduled daily pickup routes, monthly centralized billing, and consolidated shipment manifesting.

Reverse Pickup & Returns

Streamlined reverse logistics workflows designed for customer product returns, warranty repairs, merchandise exchanges, and leased asset collections.

Special Handling Enquiries

Tailored assessment and handling protocols for delicate instruments, fragile glassware, oversized cartons, or unusually sensitive shipments.

Suitable Requirement: High-value optical equipment, artwork samples, ceramics, sensitive electronics, and delicate prototypes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidelines on courier booking, parcel acceptance, tracking updates, and packaging recommendations.

Billable weight is calculated by comparing the actual physical gross weight (using certified scales) against the volumetric dimensional weight (Length × Width × Height in cm divided by 5000). The higher of the two values is applied to determine carriage charges.
Prohibited goods include flammable liquids, compressed gases, explosives, ammunition, corrosive acids, perishable food without refrigeration, currency banknotes, bearer bonds, contraband narcotics, and any items restricted under national transport safety laws.
Fragile items must be individually wrapped in dense bubble wrap with at least 5 cm of perimeter clearance inside a rigid multi-wall corrugated box. Internal void space should be filled with cushioning peanuts or air pillows so contents cannot shift during vehicle transit.
Upon package handover, the recipient acknowledges receipt via the courier’s mobile scanner screen or one-time delivery verification code. An electronic confirmation record containing the recipient name, timestamp, and geo-stamp is instantly logged.
