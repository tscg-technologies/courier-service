How Courier Delivery Works

A transparent six-stage parcel journey from booking inception to verified doorstep handover.

Illustrative Delivery Workflow — Demonstration Model
1

Booking Created

Sender enters pickup and destination addresses, package dimensions, and service preference.

2

Pickup Scheduled

A localized courier route is assigned, and a pickup window is allocated to the sender.

3

Parcel Collected

Field courier inspects weight, applies barcode label, and issues initial receipt scan.

4

Sorting & Processing

Shipment arrives at automated sorting hub and is segregated by target destination pincode.

5

Out for Delivery

Loaded onto last-mile delivery vehicle; recipient receives automated delivery notice.

6

Delivered

Handover completed with digital proof of delivery and instant confirmation notification.
